# Observer playtest guide

Give a new tester only the launch URL and permission to open the controls. Do not explain solutions or suggest a route.

Observe initial goal comprehension after two minutes; willingness to explore another entrance; readability of detection; reaction to the outage; whether carrying versus towing feels like a choice; whether ship control feels newly gained.

After a run, ask:
1. What did you want to try that the game did not allow?
2. Which consequence surprised you, and did you understand its cause?
3. Did you see another possible approach?
4. Did installing the heart feel like gaining a new capability?
5. Would you replay immediately? Why?

Record actual first-run and replay duration, deaths, lost/stuck locations, input discomfort and frame issues. Automated route tests are not human playtest evidence.
